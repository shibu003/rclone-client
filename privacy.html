Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

This OAuth client is used with rclone, an open-source command-line program, by one person to copy their own files from their own computer to their own Google Drive.

What it accesses

With the permission given on Google's consent screen, rclone reads and writes files in the Google Drive of the account that gave it. Nothing else is accessed.

Where data goes

Files move only between that person's computer and that person's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server. No data is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Other people

This client is not offered to the public. If someone else authorizes it, rclone on their own computer can reach only their own account, and none of their data reaches the developer. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
